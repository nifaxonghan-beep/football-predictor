      This is a statistical estimate (a Poisson goals model) built from the numbers shown in the boxes. It ignores injuries, lineups, tactics and luck, so treat it as a guide, not a guarantee. In backtests the rating model beat base rates but trailed bookmaker odds. Recent form nudges expected goals up for the in-form side and down for its opponent. Results are only as good as your inputs: use recent averages from a reliable stats site.
